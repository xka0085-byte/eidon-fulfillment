How it works

Day 0–1 — You order and send your photo

Through the site, WhatsApp, or email. We confirm your photo works and your all-in total — which never changes.

Day 1–4 — Made to order

Your piece is made to order — one at a time, no shelf stock. Then it's QC-checked and photographed before it leaves, and you get the photos.

Day 4–6 — Packed & shipped

Gift-ready packaging, handed to an international tracked line. Your tracking number is sent to you the same day.

Day 6–15 — On the way

7–12 days in transit to most countries, tracked the whole way, delivered to your door. If tracking stalls, we watch it and tell you.

Ordering more than one piece?

Multiple pieces ship together in one consolidated package wherever stock allows — so you pay one shipping cost instead of several. Handy if you're ordering matching pieces for the whole family.
