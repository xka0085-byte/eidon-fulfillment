FAQ

Questions dropshippers actually ask

No sales fluff — the same answers I give in DMs, written down.

How long does fulfillment take?

10–15 days door to door. Breakdown: 2–4 days for domestic sourcing and QC inside China, then 7–12 days on an international line (YunExpress-class). Anyone promising 3-day delivery from a China direct line is not being straight with you. If you need local-warehouse speed, I'll tell you that's not me.

How do I pay you from overseas?

USDT (TRC-20) — instant, no bank fees, and the savings are baked into your quote. Payoneer payment request for business customers (ACH ~1%). Wise for EU/UK/US transfers. PayPal for small trial orders. Escrow available on your first order if you want protection before trusting a stranger on the internet — which, honestly, is the right instinct.

Do you actually inspect products?

Yes, every unit: appearance, size against spec, weight, and basic function where applicable. You get photos before anything ships. If it looks wrong, it goes back to the supplier — you never see it, and you never pay international shipping for garbage.

Can you combine items from different 1688 suppliers?

Yes — consolidation is one of the main reasons to use an agent at all. Parcels from several suppliers arrive at my side, get checked, and leave as one package. You pay one international shipping fee instead of three.

What won't you handle?

Batteries and powered items, liquids, oversized freight — these need special lines I don't run. And no counterfeit branded goods, ever. The "Supreme hoodie for ¥40" on 1688 is fake, and reselling it puts your store, your payment account, and mine at risk. I refuse these jobs and you should respect that, not shop around for an agent who says yes.

What if something arrives defective?

It never reaches your customer. Defective units go back to the supplier at the supplier's cost, replacement sourcing is quoted immediately, and you get the QC photos that caught it. If a defect slips past me and reaches a customer, that's my miss — we settle it fairly and you'll see it in how I handle it.

Do you do branded packaging?

Yes — thank-you cards, logo inserts, custom tape. You supply the design, I handle printing and insertion, quoted per request. Unbranded plain packaging is the default at no extra cost.

How do we start?

Email or WhatsApp me a product link and your destination country. You get an itemized quote within 24 hours. Recommended first step: a 1–2 unit trial order — judge what I do, not what I say. If the trial goes well, we set up a simple workflow for your ongoing orders.

Question not here?

Ask me directly — replies within a few hours during China daytime.
