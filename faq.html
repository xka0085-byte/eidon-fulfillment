FAQ

Questions customers actually ask

No sales fluff — the same answers I give in chat, written down.

Why buy from a one-person store instead of Amazon?

Because the things I stock are sourced directly from Chinese suppliers and curated one by one — not the same bestseller clones listed by 40 different sellers. And because when something goes wrong (or right), you talk to the person whose name is on the store — not ticket #4821. Speed and scale are Amazon's game; direct sourcing and honesty are mine. There's no brand tax baked into my prices.

How long does shipping take?

10–15 days door to door. Breakdown: 2–4 days for sourcing and QC inside China, then 7–12 days on an international tracked line. Anyone promising 3-day delivery from a China direct line is not being straight with you. If you need local-warehouse speed, I'll tell you that's not me.

Is shipping really free everywhere?

Yes — tracked worldwide shipping is included in every price, US to Europe to wherever. No weight calculators, no checkout surprises. The price on the product page is the price you pay. (Bundles get even better value — see the Canvas Tote + Charm Bundle.)

How do I pay?

PayPal — small orders, buyer protection included. USDT (TRC-20) — instant, no bank fees. Payoneer — for business customers. Escrow — available on your first order if you want protection before trusting a new store, which is exactly the right instinct.

Do you actually inspect products?

Yes, every unit: appearance, size against spec, weight, and basic function where applicable. You get photos before anything ships. If it looks wrong, it goes back — you never see it, and you never pay international shipping for garbage.

Can I order multiple pieces?

Yes — bundles ship in one box and bundle pricing already undercuts buying separately. The Canvas Tote + Charm Bundle is the built-in example of this.

What if something arrives damaged?

It never should — but if it does: photo proof within 7 days, full refund immediately, and you keep the item. Replacement re-shipping is free if you still want the product. If a defect slips past me and reaches a customer, that's my miss — we settle it fairly and you'll see it in how I handle it.

Do you do gift packaging?

Yes — thank-you cards, custom notes, and clean gift-ready packaging on every order. Quoted free, always.

How do we start?

Tap Order via WhatsApp on any product, use the chat box, or email me. You get an exact total within a few hours — and everything is covered by the 30-Day Keep-It Guarantee.
