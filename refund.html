30-Day Guarantee

If you're not happy, you get your money back. Period.

Here's our full policy in plain English — including the part where we don't ask you to ship anything back to China.

💚 Damaged or wrong item?

Send us a photo within 7 days of delivery. Full refund immediately — and keep the item (or throw it away, your call). We'll also re-ship the correct product free of charge if you still want it. This is our mistake, and we treat it that way.

🤷 Not what you expected?

Color looked different, vibe wasn't right, changed your mind — whatever the reason, full refund within 30 days of delivery. No interrogation, no forms. And no, you don't need to ship it back.

📦 Lost in transit?

If tracking shows no movement for 30+ days after dispatch, full refund — no questions. We watch every tracking number ourselves, so we'll usually tell you before you ask.

"Wait — I don't have to ship it back to China?"

Correct. Here's the honest reason: international return shipping costs $20–40, which is more than most of our items are worth. Making you spend $30 to return a $18 product, wait 3 weeks, then restock something nobody can resell — that's a policy designed to protect the seller, not the customer. We'd rather eat the item cost and refund you instantly. You keep the product (gift it to a friend), we refund the money, everybody moves on.

For orders above $60 where a return genuinely makes sense, contact us — we'll work out a partial refund or a fair solution case by case. We've never had a fight we couldn't settle with a refund and an apology.

How to claim

Message us

Email or WhatsApp with your order number and a photo (for damage/wrong item cases). One message, no forms.

We verify fast

Same day or next. We keep QC photos of every order on file, so verification is quick on our side too.

Refund lands

Refunded to your original payment method — PayPal, card, USDT, wherever it came from. PayPal refunds typically appear within 24–48h of processing.

💡 Why so generous? Simple: we QC-inspect every item before it leaves China, so damage and wrong items should be rare. If we ever have to refund you, that's our quality process telling us something — we'd rather hear it loud. Refunds happen on our dime, and we keep the item on yours.

Ready when you are

Every order ships with QC photos, full tracking, and this guarantee behind it.
