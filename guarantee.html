30-Day Keep-It Guarantee

If you're not happy, you get your money back. Period.

Here's the full policy in plain English — including the part where we don't ask you to ship anything back to China.

💚 Damaged or wrong item?

Send us a photo within 7 days of delivery. Full refund immediately — and keep the item (or throw it away, your call). We'll also re-ship the correct product free of charge if you still want it.

🤷 Not what you expected?

Color looked different, vibe wasn't right, changed your mind — whatever the reason, full refund within 30 days of delivery. No interrogation, no forms. And no, you don't need to ship it back.

📦 Lost in transit?

If tracking shows no movement for 30+ days after dispatch, full refund — no questions. We watch every tracking number ourselves, so we'll usually tell you before you ask.

"Wait — I don't have to ship it back to China?"

Correct. Here's the honest reason: international return shipping costs $20–40, which is more than most of our items are worth. Making you spend $30 to return a $34 product, wait three weeks, then restock something nobody can resell — that's a policy designed to protect the seller, not the customer. We'd rather eat the item cost and refund you instantly.

For orders above $60 where a return genuinely makes sense, contact us — we'll work out a partial refund or a fair solution case by case. We've never had a disagreement we couldn't settle with a refund and an apology.

How to claim

Message us

Email or WhatsApp with your order number and a photo (for damage or wrong-item cases). One message, no forms.

We verify fast

Same day or next. Your QC photos and the tracking record are on file, so verification is quick: your photo of what arrived, plus what we checked before it shipped.

Refund lands

Refunded to your original payment method — PayPal, card, USDT, wherever it came from. PayPal refunds typically appear within 24–48h of processing.

💡 Why so generous? Two honest reasons. First, international returns cost more than the product. Second, a store like this survives on trust — if we ever have to refund you, we'd rather fix it fast and keep you as a customer than win an argument and lose you.

Ready when you are

Every order ships tracked worldwide, with this guarantee behind it.
