QC-First Guarantee

You approve it before it ships.

Every piece is made to order, checked, and photographed before it leaves. Nothing ships until you see your actual piece and say it looks right. Here's exactly what that means — including what we can't do.

We make your piece and QC-check it

Your order is made, then inspected against what you sent us — the photo, the print, the spelling, the finish.

We send you photos of your actual piece

Real photos, not stock images. You see exactly what will arrive before it's in the mail.

You approve — or we fix it first

Something off? We remake it before shipping, at no cost to you. Only after your OK does it leave.

The guarantee, by product type

Custom keepsakes

Everything made with your photo, print, name or message.

  • Cancel any time before we send your QC photos — full refund, no questions.
  • Once approved, personalized pieces can't be returned for a change of mind — they were made for you alone and can't be resold.
  • Arrives damaged, wrong, or different from the photos you approved? Full refund or a free remake within 14 days of delivery — and you keep the piece. This is our mistake and we own it.

Standard items

Decor and everyday essentials with no personalization.

  • 14 days from delivery to request a return — unused, in its original packaging.
  • Return shipping is on you — or take a partial refund and keep the item. Often simpler for everyone.
  • Arrives damaged or wrong? Full refund within 14 days, no return needed.

What isn't covered

We'd rather say this upfront than argue about it later. Not covered:

  • Change of mind after you've approved the QC photos — that's the moment the piece becomes yours.
  • Wrong photo, name, or spelling that you supplied. We check with you before making it, but the final approval is yours.
  • Normal wear, accidental damage after delivery, or misuse.
  • Minor differences between your screen and the finished piece — photos on steel render slightly differently than on a phone.
  • Standard items damaged after delivery, or returned without original packaging.

How to claim

Message us

WhatsApp or email with your order number and a photo of what arrived. One message, no forms.

We verify fast

Same day or next. Your QC photos and the tracking record are on file, so verification takes minutes.

Refund or remake lands

Refunded to your original payment method — PayPal typically within 24–48h of processing; crypto refunds are sent in USDT to your address.

Why it works this way. Two honest reasons: personalized pieces can't be resold, and international return shipping costs more than most of our items. So instead of checking after the fact, we check before shipping — you approve your piece, and we catch mistakes before they cost anyone a return. Same protection, less waste.

Ready when you are

Made to order, approved by you, shipped worldwide with tracking.
