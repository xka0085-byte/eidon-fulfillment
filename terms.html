These terms cover orders placed with EveryBeloved. They're meant to be readable, not to trap you — if anything here is unclear, ask us before ordering.

Made to order

Every piece is made to order with the photo or print you send. Because each piece is individual, production starts once you confirm your photo. If you need to change or cancel an order, message us as early as possible — before production starts, we'll refund in full; after it starts, we'll tell you honestly where things stand.

Your photo

By sending a photo you confirm it's yours to use. We make your piece as instructed and keep the photo only as long as needed for your order — see the Privacy Policy. We never publish your photos without asking you first.

Prices and payment

Prices are in USD and include free tracked worldwide shipping — the price on the product page is the total. Payment via PayPal, USDT, Payoneer, or escrow, as described on the Shipping & Payment page. Orders are produced after payment is confirmed.

Shipping times

10–15 days door to door is our honest estimate (2–4 days made to order, 7–12 days international tracked transit). Delivery times depend on carriers and customs; we can't control delays once a parcel is in the line, but we'll always tell you what we know.

The QC-First Guarantee

The QC-First Guarantee

Custom pieces: you approve QC photos of your actual piece before it ships. Cancel for a full refund any time before those photos are sent. After approval, personalized pieces are not returnable for a change of mind — they were made for you alone. If an item arrives damaged, wrong, or different from the photos you approved, you get a full refund or a free remake within 14 days of delivery, and you keep the item.

Standard items (no personalization): 14 days from delivery to request a return — unused, in original packaging, return shipping at your cost — or take a partial refund and keep the item. Damaged or wrong on arrival: full refund within 14 days, no return needed.

Lost in transit (no tracking movement for 30+ days after dispatch): full refund either way. Full details on the Guarantee page.

Getting your piece right

Made-to-order pieces are rendered from your photo — a close, sharp, well-lit photo gives the best result. Small differences between the screen and the finished piece are normal. If a piece arrives clearly wrong (wrong photo, wrong name, damage), that's covered by the guarantee.

Ashes and fur

Keepsake chambers ship empty for you to fill at home. We never ask you to mail cremated remains or fur to us, and we don't handle them on your behalf.

Liability

Our liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it. We're not liable for indirect damages or for delays caused by carriers, customs, or events outside our control.
