Pricing

Two numbers. That's the whole pricing.

For every product you sell, you get one all-in unit price from me — goods, sourcing work, and domestic China leg already inside. Then international shipping by weight. Add your margin, list your product. No separate "service fee" line, no repacking charges, no surprises.

What's insideNotes

Illustrative format (numbers change per product): phone case 1688 ¥18 → unit price $4.20 · stainless necklace ¥32 → unit price $7.60 · desk gadget ¥85 → unit price $17.40. Every quote is itemized if you want to see inside.

Why it's a separate line instead of baked into the unit price: weight changes with consolidation — when you combine three items into one parcel, you pay one shipping fee instead of three. Baking it in would mean overpaying. Every quote confirms the exact rate before you pay, and when my freight volume grows, my contract rates drop — the difference passes through to you.
