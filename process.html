Process

From your product link to your customer's door

Six steps, fully transparent. You always know where your order is and what happens next.

You send the product (Day 0)

A 1688/Taobao link, a photo, or just a description. Any format works — I'll find it, or find who makes it.

You get an itemized quote (within 24h)

Goods price at actual 1688 cost, my service fee, domestic courier, international shipping by weight — every line visible. Nothing proceeds until you approve.

You pay, I buy (Day 1–2)

USDT, Payoneer, Wise, PayPal for small orders, or escrow for your first order. The moment funds land, I place the order with the supplier.

Arrival & QC (Day 3–6)

The parcel reaches me from the supplier. I inspect every unit — appearance, size, weight, function — and send you photos. Problem units go back to the supplier, not to your customer.

Pack & ship (Day 6–8)

Proper repacking, your branding if requested, consolidation with other items if you have several. Handed to a YunExpress-class international line. Tracking number issued.

Tracked to the door (Day 8–15)

Tracking updates are forwarded to you automatically — you don't have to chase me for status. Typical door-to-door: 10–15 days from order to your customer.

💡 For Shopify/WooCommerce sellers: tracking numbers can be synced back to your store automatically — one less manual step. Ask about it when we set up your account.
⚠️ Honest limits: I don't ship batteries, liquids, or oversized freight, I don't touch counterfeit branded goods, and I won't promise 3-day delivery. If your product or expectation doesn't fit what I do well, I'll say so upfront — a fulfilled "no" beats a broken "yes".
