About

One person. Chengdu, China. Direct from source.

I run Eidon on my own — sourcing accessories directly from suppliers in China and shipping them to you worldwide. No warehouse, no brand markup, no invented story.

Every order is handled by me end to end: I place the order with the supplier, the piece arrives and gets inspected, I send you photos, then it ships. You talk to the person who packs it — not a ticket queue.

What that means for you, concretely:

  • Sourced directly from Chinese suppliers — the same supply base behind most accessories you already buy
  • Prices without a brand tax — what you see is close to landed cost plus a small, open margin
  • A real human answering — message the store and you reach the person who packs your order
  • Honest timelines — 10–15 days from China, tracked the whole way

What I won't do

  • Pretend shipping is 3 days when it's 10–15.
  • Hide fees in your checkout. If my price changes, you'll see why.
Why trust a one-person store? Because every order gets my full attention — you're not ticket #4821. I'd rather earn your repeat business by how each order is handled than by a slogan. — Eidon
