About

Run by Eidon, from Chengdu.

I'm Eidon, and EveryBeloved is my store — keepsakes for the pets we love, whether they're still here or already missed. Every order is handled personally by me: you message, I answer, and your piece is QC-checked and photographed before it ships.

I started EveryBeloved for one reason: too many shops sell the same stock paw icon wrapped in a story about heritage and handcraft. I wanted the opposite — your pet's actual face, their actual print, their actual words — at a fair price, with an honest answer about how long it takes.

I also think the small companions get shortchanged. Hamsters, birds, fish, reptiles — their people are often told it was "just a pet". It wasn't. We take every loving owner — and every beloved pet — seriously.

What you can count on

  • Your piece is QC-checked and photographed before it ships — you see the photos
  • I handle every order personally, from your first message to the tracking number
  • Made to wear every day — close-ups of the real finish on every product page
  • Free tracked shipping worldwide, 10–15 days, already in the price
  • QC-First Guarantee — if anything arrives wrong, full refund and you keep the piece

What I won't do

  • Invent a heritage. There's no "three generations of jewelers". There's one person, in Chengdu.
  • Promise 3-day delivery. It's 10–15 days, tracked, and I'd rather tell you the truth upfront.
  • Hide fees. The price on the product page is the price you pay — shipping is already inside it.
Questions before you order? Message me — WhatsApp or email. I answer personally, usually within a few hours. — Eidon
