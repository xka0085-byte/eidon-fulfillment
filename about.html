About

One person. Chengdu, China. No middlemen.

I'm Eidon — a developer based in Chengdu who got tired of watching simple accessories get marked up 5–10× between a factory in China and a storefront in the US.

So I cut the chain. I source original-design pieces directly from 1688 and Chinese factories — the same platforms that supply half the "brands" you already buy from — inspect every piece before it ships, and send it to you straight. No warehouse markup, no brand tax, no story invented by a marketing team.

What that means for you, concretely:

  • Designs you won't find in every mall store — I pick from original designers, not bestseller clones
  • Prices that make sense — my margin is a few dollars per piece, and the math is visible on the shipping page
  • A real human answering — when you message this store, you're talking to the person who packs your order
  • Honest timelines — 10–15 days from China, tracked the whole way. I'd rather tell you that upfront than promise 3 days and deliver 12

What I won't do

  • Pretend shipping is 3 days when it's 10–15.
  • Hide fees in your checkout. If my price changes, you'll see why.
Why trust a one-person store? Fair question. Because "one person" means every order gets my full attention — you're not ticket #4821 in a queue. And my first customers get the kind of care that only happens when someone is building a reputation from zero. I intend to earn yours. — Eidon
