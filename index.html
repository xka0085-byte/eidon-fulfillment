How it works

From your photo to your door in 10–15 days.

No marketplace, no ticket queue. The whole process:

You choose a piece and send your photo

A close-up of their face, or a clear photo of their paw or nose print. Order online or message us — we'll confirm the photo works before anything is made.

It's made for you, one at a time

No shelf stock, no batch run — yours is the only one. Then it's QC-checked and photographed before it leaves, and you get the photos.

It ships to you, tracked

Packed and handed to an international tracked line. 7–12 days to most countries, worldwide. Free — it's already in the price.

You approve it before it ships

We QC-check your piece, photograph it, and send you the photos. Something off? We remake it free. Damaged or wrong after that — full refund or free remake within 14 days. One message, no forms.

Not sure your photo will work? Send it to us before ordering — we'll check it free and tell you honestly whether it'll make a good piece, and which of your photos would work better.

Common questions

Before you order.

What photo should I send?
A close-up of their face — sharp, well-lit, eyes visible. For paw prints, a straight-on photo of the print in daylight. If it won't work well, we tell you before anything is made. Full photo guide →
Can I see my piece before it ships?
Yes — every order is QC-checked and photographed, and you approve the photos before it leaves. If anything looks off, we remake it before shipping at no cost.
How long does it take?
10–15 days door to door: a few days to make your piece, then 7–12 days on an international tracked line. Free shipping is included.
Which pets can you make keepsakes for?
Any of them — dogs, cats, rabbits, hamsters, birds, horses, reptiles, fish. If you can photograph the mark they left, we can make it. More questions →
