How it works

From your photo to your door in 10–15 days.

No marketplace, no ticket queue. The whole process:

You choose a piece and send your photo

A close-up of their face, or a clear photo of their paw or nose print. Order through the site or message us on WhatsApp — we'll confirm the photo works before anything is made.

It's made for you, one at a time

Your piece is made to order — there's no shelf stock and no batch run. Yours is the only one. Then it's QC-checked and photographed before it leaves.

It ships to you, tracked

Packed and handed to an international tracked line. 7–12 days to most countries, worldwide. Free — it's already in the price.

If anything's wrong, we fix it

Damaged, wrong, or just not right — the 30-Day Keep-It Guarantee means a full refund and you keep the piece. One message, no forms.

Straight talk

What we promise — and what we won't pretend.

What we promise

  • Your piece is QC-checked and photographed before it ships
  • Every order is handled personally by Eidon, in Chengdu
  • 316L stainless steel: hypoallergenic, water-resistant, no flaking plating
  • Free tracked shipping, worldwide — already in the price
  • 30-Day Keep-It Guarantee: full refund, you keep the piece

What we don't claim

  • A "family jewellers since 19XX" story — there is no invented heritage
  • 3-day delivery — it's 10–15 days, and we'd rather say so
  • Hidden fees — the price on the product page is the price you pay

We take every loving owner — and every beloved pet — seriously. Big or small, fur, feather, or fin: the mark they left matters, and we treat your order that way.
